How we work - workflow

Every plan starts with a content and structure review, custom layouts and a lean, modern WordPress build.

01 — Discovery

Intent, audience, constraints

We map goals, content and guardrails, then set a lean, realistic scope that matches your resources and timeline.

Detailed focus: Content & structure

  • Content and page mapping before design
  • Navigation and hierarchy that match your project
  • Wireframes / low-fidelity layouts where needed
  • Simple content guidelines (tone, length, image ratios)

02 — Design

System & composition

Grid, type, components. Editorial clarity over noise, with layouts tuned to your photography and visual language.

Detailed focus: Design & front-end

  • Custom layout and type system (no generic theme skins)
  • Responsive grids, balanced margins and rhythm
  • Gentle motion where it helps pacing (no circus)
  • Design tuned to your existing brand assets and photography

03 — Build

From prototype to live site

We build a custom theme with the right tools for your project (Pinegrow, Kadence, block editor), keeping the stack lean and documented.

Detailed focus: Build & handover

  • Modern block-based WordPress build (Kadence + custom blocks)
  • Performance-minded decisions (caching, images, fonts)
  • Basic SEO and analytics setup

04 — Launch & care

Deploy, optimise, support

We launch, run basic performance and analytics passes, and—if you want us on call—plug you into a care plan with hosting, updates and strategic time.

Detailed focus: Launch & support

  • Handover session so you can update comfortably
  • Short, practical documentation (no 40-page PDF)
  • Optional ongoing care plans with hosting included
