how we think and build

What gets built depends on the clarity of the decisions behind it.

Structure

Architecture, content, navigation and key decisions — including what to build, and what to leave out. Nothing is designed until the structure is clear.

Design & Development

Custom layout, type system and visual logic — no generic themes. Each component is built to the structure, with attention to performance, balance and precision.

Evolution

Digital systems are not finished at launch. Content shifts, priorities change, pages are added. The structure is built to accommodate this — not to resist it.

Care

Handover, documentation and — where needed — ongoing support. The system is yours. We make sure you can use it with confidence, and that someone is on hand when things need attention.

Hosting & Care

Every WordPress site needs ongoing technical maintenance — updates, backups, security checks. The question is not whether, but who handles it.

You can manage your own hosting, provided it meets a minimum quality standard we agree on together. Or we manage everything under one of our care plans, which include hosting as part of the service.

Either way, the site remains yours.

Self-managed

You handle hosting. We remain available.

You host the site with your own provider. We will advise on minimum requirements — server environment, caching, SSL, backup policy — so the site continues to perform as built. Technical maintenance is your responsibility, though we remain available for agreed support when needed.

What this includes

  • Hosting requirements brief at project handover
  • One post-launch technical check (30 days)
  • Documentation so your team can manage day-to-day updates
  • Support available separately, on request

Managed care

From €60 / month — hosting included

We manage the full technical layer of your site — hosting, updates, security, backups — on our managed infrastructure via SiteGround or Hostinger. You focus on the work. We ensure the system stays stable, secure and current.

What this includes

  • Managed hosting on EU-based servers (SiteGround / Hostinger)
  • Regular WordPress core, theme & plugin updates
  • Daily backups with rolling retention
  • Security monitoring, firewall & malware checks
  • Performance checks & uptime monitoring
  • 2 hours / month content credit — page edits, text updates, image swaps (not new features)
  • One strategic check-in per year

Scope and pricing vary by project complexity. Full details available on request.
